# Poincaré Conjecture

> **Question.** Is every simply connected, closed three-dimensional manifold a three-sphere?

**Status:** solved by Grigori Perelman (2002–2003), using Richard Hamilton's Ricci flow with surgery. The Clay Mathematics Institute awarded the Millennium Prize in 2010; Perelman declined it. [Clay press release](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Poincare-press-release.pdf)

## Statement

Every simply connected, closed (compact, without boundary) three-manifold is homeomorphic to the three-sphere $S^3$.

## The landscape

Perelman proved Thurston's more general **geometrization conjecture**, which classifies all closed three-manifolds; the Poincaré conjecture is a special case. The higher-dimensional analogues had been settled earlier (Smale for $n \ge 5$, Freedman for $n = 4$ in the topological category).
