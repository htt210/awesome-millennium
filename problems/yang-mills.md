# Yang–Mills Existence and Mass Gap

> **Question.** Does four-dimensional quantum Yang–Mills theory exist as a mathematically rigorous quantum field theory, and does it have a mass gap?

**Status:** open. [Clay Mathematics Institute](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/) · [official problem description (Jaffe–Witten), in *The Millennium Prize Problems*](https://www.claymath.org/library/monographs/MPPc.pdf)

## Statement

For every compact simple gauge group $G$, prove that a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory exists on $\mathbb{R}^4$, satisfying axioms at least as strong as the Wightman (or Osterwalder–Schrader) axioms, and that it has a **mass gap** $\Delta \gt 0$: every state orthogonal to the vacuum has energy at least $\Delta$.

## The landscape

- **Asymptotic freedom and confinement** are understood physically and numerically (lattice gauge theory) but not rigorously in four dimensions.
- **Lower-dimensional models** — for example two-dimensional nonlinear sigma models, which are also asymptotically free and are expected to have a mass gap — are the standard rigorous testing ground.
